Expansion: Verified Engagement & Relationship Architecture
Brand Name	Expansion	Relevance & Rationale
NEXA	Newsletter Enterprise eXperience Architecture	Directly references newsletter and enterprise architecture. Short, modern, and easy to trademark. Slightly less governance-focused than VERA.
CREO	Creator Relationship Enterprise OS	Emphasizes the creator-first experience while signaling enterprise operating-system depth. “Creo” means “I create” in Latin—a subtle nod to the creator economy.
GOVOS	GOVerned Operating System	Very explicit about governance and CMMI Level 5. Strong for regulated industries and enterprise procurement, but sounds more infrastructure than creator-friendly.
TRUST	Trusted Relationship & Unified Subscriber Tool	Immediately communicates the platform’s core differentiator—trust and governance over subscriber data. Slightly generic and harder to trademark.
AXIOM	Audience eXperience & Intelligence Operating Model	Sounds enterprise-grade and analytical. Emphasizes the data platform, KPI framework, and AI governance. Less directly tied to newsletters and creators.
